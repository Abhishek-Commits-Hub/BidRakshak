# BIDRAKSHAK

### AI-Assisted Bid Compliance Verification Platform for Government Procurement

> **AI assists. Rules validate. Evidence supports. Human authority decides.**

BIDRAKSHAK is our prototype for making bid verification more structured, transparent, and easier to review.

In a typical procurement process, an officer may have to go through a tender document, understand multiple requirements, check documents submitted by a bidder, compare values, identify missing information, investigate inconsistencies, and finally record a decision.

We built BIDRAKSHAK around a simple idea:

**Instead of making the officer search through everything manually, bring the requirement, its supporting evidence, the validation result, and the officer's decision together in one place.**

# 🎯 The Problem

Bid verification is not just about checking whether a document exists.

An officer may need to answer questions such as:

- What exactly does the tender require?
- Which document proves that requirement?
- What value was found in that document?
- Does that value satisfy the requirement?
- Is any information missing or inconsistent?
- Does something need clarification?
- Why was a particular decision made?

When this information is spread across multiple documents, the verification process can become repetitive and difficult to track.

BIDRAKSHAK is designed around one connected workflow:

```text
Requirement
     ↓
Evidence
     ↓
Validation
     ↓
Finding
     ↓
Officer Review
     ↓
Decision
